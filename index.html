About CollabAI

CollabAI is a creator-brand discovery marketplace demo designed to help both sides find relevant, authentic collaboration opportunities using AI-assisted ranking.

What we check

Audience authenticity, engagement consistency, brand safety, identity signals, campaign fit and historical performance indicators.

Terms & Conditions

By using this demo platform, users agree to provide accurate information, respect intellectual property, avoid spam and follow applicable laws. Collaboration agreements remain between brands and creators.

Marketplace use

AI scores are decision-support indicators and not guarantees. Users should complete independent due diligence before payment or campaign activation.

Privacy Policy

We collect account, profile, campaign and usage data required to operate the marketplace. Sensitive contact details are shown only after a profile is unlocked or a request is accepted.

Data choices

Users may request access, correction or deletion of their account information through the complaint page.

Refund Policy

Subscription and sponsorship payments may be eligible for review where services were not delivered. Creator campaign payments follow the agreed milestone and dispute terms.

Refund requests
